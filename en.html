Bonafit Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

At a glance

  • There is no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run any servers.
  • Your location, health data, and workout records are stored only on your phone and watch and are never sent to the developer.
  • The app uses no ads, no analytics tools, and no tracking, and never sells your information.

Bonafit (“the app”) is a workout tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch, and for Android phones and Wear OS watches. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The developer of the app (“the developer”) complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws.

1. Information the app uses and why

All of the information below is processed only on your devices. The developer does not collect or receive it. Each item is used only after you allow the related permission in your operating system.

InformationWhen and why
Location (GPS) Used only while you record a run, to measure distance, pace, and route. During a run, recording continues even when the screen is off (shown by the location indicator on iPhone and by a notification on Android). Location use stops when the run ends, and the route is saved with that run.
Reading health data
Apple Health / Health Connect		 Reads steps, walking distance, heart rate, and body composition recorded by other apps such as InBody (weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass, height) to show them on the walking and body composition screens and to estimate calories burned.
Writing health data
Apple Health (iPhone, Apple Watch)		 Saves runs recorded on Apple Watch to the Health app as workouts. During a run, the watch reads heart rate and distance to show them on screen.
Height and weight Entered by you or taken from an InBody result, and used to estimate calories burned for each workout.
Workout records Squat reps and sets, walks, runs (time, distance, route), and records you add manually, saved to show your history and statistics.
Camera and photos When you take or pick a photo of an InBody result sheet, on-device text recognition (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android) reads the numbers. Photos are not kept. On Android, a photo taken with the camera stays in the app's private temporary folder until your next capture, when it is deleted. The numbers read are saved as a body composition record.
Motion sensors The accelerometer is used to count squats. Sensor readings are not stored; only a squat depth calibration value is saved.
Notifications and Live Activities Show progress during runs and walks as a notification (Android) or a Live Activity on the Lock Screen (iPhone), and give interval cues with sound and vibration.

2. Where data is stored and for how long

  • All information is stored in the app's private storage on your phone and watch, and is never sent to the developer's servers or any external storage.
  • It stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Data saved in Apple Health or Health Connect is managed by those apps, where you can review and delete it.
  • If device backup (iCloud or Google backup) is turned on, app data may be included in your backup and is handled under Apple's or Google's policies.

3. Transfers between your phone and watch

Workout records, height and weight, and in-app messages are exchanged only between your phone and its paired watch, using WatchConnectivity on Apple devices and the Google Wearable Data Layer on Android devices. They never pass through the developer's servers.

4. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not share, sell, or outsource the processing of your information. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or sold.

The app does use the following platform features:

  • Apple HealthKit and Google Health Connect: only the health data you allow is read or written. Use of data received from Health Connect complies with the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Google ML Kit (Android): text recognition runs on the device, and photos and recognized text are not sent anywhere. Google may collect usage statistics such as device and app information and performance metrics to improve the service. See the Google ML Kit Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Deleting your data

The developer does not keep any of your information, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. You can delete your data at any time:

  • Delete individual workout records and InBody results from the history screens in the app
  • Deleting the app removes all app data from the device
  • Delete health data in Apple Health or Health Connect

6. Your rights

  • You can turn off location, health, camera, and notification permissions at any time in your device settings or on the app's Permissions screen. Only the related feature stops working.
  • You can view, edit, and delete your saved records directly in the app.
  • For questions or requests, contact us at the address below, and we will respond without delay.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and the developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Security

As a rule, information never leaves your devices. App data is kept in storage that the operating system isolates and protects for each app. Setting a device passcode or biometric lock adds further protection.
